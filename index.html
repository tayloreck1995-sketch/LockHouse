About LockHouse

LockHouse Escape Room offers spine-chilling puzzles, immersive rooms, and unforgettable adventures. Bring your friends, test your wits, and escape before time runs out!

Experience the Thrill

  • The Circus
  • The Time Traveler’s Study
  • The Game Room

    • The Circus – The star performer has vanished just before the big show, and the fate of the circus rests in your hands. Search backstage tents, unlock hidden compartments, and solve mechanical puzzles disguised as circus games to uncover sabotage and find the missing performer before the curtain rises in 60 minutes. Can you save the show?

    The Time Traveler’s Study – A brilliant scientist has disappeared after inventing a time machine. Match clues from the past, present, and future, operate mysterious devices, and solve paradox-based puzzles to restore the timeline. Fail, and history itself could erase you!

    The Game Room – Enter a mysterious room filled with games of every kind— Solve clever riddles, unlock secret compartments, and outsmart the enchanted games before time runs out, or risk becoming a permanent player in the room’s endless challenges
