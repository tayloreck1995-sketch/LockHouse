About LockHouse

LockHouse Escape Room offers spine-chilling puzzles, immersive rooms, and unforgettable adventures. Bring your friends, test your wits, and escape before time runs out!

Experience the Thrill

  • The Classroom
  • The Time Traveler’s Study
  • The Morgue

    • The Classroom - You are a teacher assigned to follow up on a students misbehavior habits. Turns out one of your students has turned your classroom into a puzzle just for you to solve. A fun and friendly experience for all ages!

    The Time Traveler’s Study – A brilliant scientist has disappeared after inventing a time machine. Match clues from the past, present, and future, operate mysterious devices, and solve paradox-based puzzles to restore the timeline. Fail, and history itself could erase you!

    The Morgue - There has been a string of disappearences in the area. As the top detective in the County you have been called in to uncover the truth behind a kidnapper and a mad doctor. Be warned that the suspect may come back to erase all your hard work. Can you unwravel the mystery?
