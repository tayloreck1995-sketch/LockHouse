About LockHouse

LockHouse Escape Room offers spine-chilling puzzles, immersive rooms, and unforgettable adventures. Bring your friends, test your wits, and experience the thrill together! Perfect for work events, corporate team building, date nights, family outings, and group celebrations — LockHouse is where teamwork, communication, and creativity come to life!

Experience the Thrill

  • The Mad Tortuga

    You and your crew have boarded the infamous pirate vessel, The Mad Tortuga, in search of hidden treasure—only to find yourselves trapped below deck! Uncover the captain’s secrets and navigate treacherous puzzles to escape before the ship sets sail forever. A swashbuckling adventure for all ages!

  • The Time Traveler’s Study

    A close friend also apart of the Time Travelers association has disappeared after traveling back and forward through time. The time machine is set to self destruct in 1 hour! Crack the security codes to get to the time machine and shut it off before it self destructs! Fail, and history itself could erase you!

  • The Investigation

    There has been a string of disappearances in the area. As the top detective in the county, you’ve been called in to uncover the truth behind a kidnapper and a mad doctor. Be warned—the suspect may come back to erase all your hard work. Can you unravel the mystery?
