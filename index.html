About LockHouse

LockHouse Escape Room offers spine-chilling puzzles, immersive rooms, and unforgettable adventures. Bring your friends, test your wits, and escape before time runs out!

Experience the Thrill

  • The Circus
  • The Pirateship
  • The Time Traveler’s Study
  • The Haunted Hotel

    • The Circus – The star performer has vanished just before the big show, and the fate of the circus rests in your hands. Search backstage tents, unlock hidden compartments, and solve mechanical puzzles disguised as circus games to uncover sabotage and find the missing performer before the curtain rises in 60 minutes. Can you save the show?

    The Pirateship – Captured by ruthless pirates and thrown in the brig, you must escape, recover their hidden royal treasure, and take control of the ship before it’s scuttled. Navigate rope-and-pulley systems, decipher nautical riddles, and uncover secret messages. Adventure and danger await at every turn!

    The Time Traveler’s Study – A brilliant scientist has disappeared after inventing a time machine. Match clues from the past, present, and future, operate mysterious devices, and solve paradox-based puzzles to restore the timeline. Fail, and history itself could erase you!

    The Haunted Hotel – Step into a 1920s hotel abandoned after a mysterious fire, where restless spirits still linger. Search flickering rooms, piece together clues, and solve spirit-based puzzles to uncover the truth—and escape before the ghosts trap you forever.

    Four worlds. Four challenges. One goal: beat the clock and conquer the mystery. Perfect for friends, families, or colleagues, LockHouse Escape Room offers immersive experiences where teamwork, cleverness, and courage lead to unforgettable victories. Are you ready to play?
