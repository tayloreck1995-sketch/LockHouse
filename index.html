About LockHouse

LockHouse Escape Room offers spine-chilling puzzles, immersive rooms, and unforgettable adventures. Bring your friends, test your wits, and experience the thrill together! Perfect for work events, corporate team building, date nights, family outings, and group celebrations — LockHouse is where teamwork, communication, and creativity come to life!

Experience the Thrill

  • The Classroom

    You are a teacher assigned to follow up on a student’s misbehavior habits. Turns out one of your students has turned your classroom into a puzzle just for you to solve. A fun and friendly experience for all ages!

  • The Time Traveler’s Study

    A close friend also apart of the Time Travlers association has disappeared after travleing back and forward through time. The time machine is set to self distruct in 1 hour! Crack the security codes to get to the time machine and shut it off before it self distructs! Fail, and history itself could erase you!

  • The Investigation

    There has been a string of disappearances in the area. As the top detective in the county, you’ve been called in to uncover the truth behind a kidnapper and a mad doctor. Be warned—the suspect may come back to erase all your hard work. Can you unravel the mystery?
