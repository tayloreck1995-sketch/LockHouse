Welcome to LockHouse Escape Room!

We're a passionate team dedicated to delivering unforgettable, fully immersive escape room experiences. Whether you're planning a team-building event, a fun family puzzle-solving adventure, or an exciting first date, we've got you covered.

Every one of our thoughtfully designed rooms challenges your wits, creativity, and teamwork, pushing you to think outside the box as the clock ticks down. Get ready to laugh, strategize, and put your cleverness to the test — because at LockHouse, the only way out is to escape.

Reservations and Bookings

How do I make a reservation?
Visit our website and click the "Book Now" button. You will be redirected to our booking site, where you can view availability and reserve your escape room.
Can I book online or over the phone?
Reservations should be made through our online booking site. Phone inquiries are welcome for questions about party room rentals and special events.
How far in advance should I book?
Online reservations can be made up to one month in advance. Please contact us if you have questions about bookings outside this timeframe.
Can I change my reservation date or time?
Reservation changes may be made up to 12 hours before your scheduled booking. Please contact us for assistance.
What is your cancellation policy?
Please contact us for information about cancellations and applicable policies. Reservation changes may be made up to 12 hours before your scheduled booking.
What happens if I'm running late?
Please call us as soon as possible if you are running late. We can discuss your reservation and determine whether accommodations are possible.
Can I add more people to my reservation?
Yes, additional participants may be added when you arrive, provided the room does not exceed its maximum capacity.
Can I book multiple rooms at once?
Groups may reserve up to two rooms. Please call (661) 505-3566 for information about larger groups or additional reservations.

Groupon and Vouchers

Do you accept Groupon vouchers?
Yes! We accept Groupon vouchers.
If I bought a Groupon, how do I use it?
Please call us directly at (661) 505-3566 to check availability and arrange your reservation. Bring your Groupon voucher and have it ready to be scanned when you arrive.
Do I need a reservation before redeeming my Groupon?
Yes. Contact us in advance to check availability and arrange your reservation. Please wait until you arrive before redeeming your Groupon so we can scan your voucher.
Can I show my Groupon on my phone?
Yes! You may show your Groupon on your phone or bring a printed copy, provided the voucher includes a scannable code or barcode.
What if my Groupon code isn't working?
Please contact Groupon Customer Support for technical assistance. Unfortunately, LockHouse Escape Room cannot modify Groupon voucher prices or resolve technical issues within Groupon's system.
Does my Groupon have an expiration date?
Groupon vouchers may have expiration dates and other restrictions. Please review the terms and conditions listed on your voucher before purchasing.
Can I use multiple Groupons for one group?
Only one Groupon may be used per group purchase. Please contact us if you have questions about your group's eligibility.

Pricing and Payments

How much does an escape room cost per person?
Pricing may vary depending on the room, date, and availability. Please visit our online booking site for current prices.
Do you accept cash and credit cards?
We accept debit cards and credit cards through our online booking site and at the door. Cash is also accepted for in-person payments. At this time, we do not accept PayPal.
Can everyone in my group pay separately?
Yes! Participants may pay separately. Please coordinate your group's booking and payment arrangements in advance.
Do you offer group discounts?
Group pricing may be available for parties of 10 or more. Please call (661) 505-3566 for more information.
Do you offer birthday party packages?
Yes! Please call (661) 505-3566 to ask about birthday party packages and available rental options.

Escape Room Experiences

What escape rooms do you currently offer?
We currently offer three escape room experiences:
  • Mad Tortuga: A pirate-themed adventure.
  • The Investigation: A mystery-solving experience.
  • The Time Traveler's Study: An adventure centered around time travel.
How long does each escape room take?
Each escape room has a 60-minute time limit. Participants also have access to three clues to help them progress through the game.
How many people can participate in one room?
The maximum group size varies by room. Please check our online booking site for the participant limits listed for each experience.
Do children need to be accompanied by an adult?
Yes. Children under the age of 13 must be accompanied by at least one adult.
Do we need previous escape room experience?
Not at all! No previous escape room experience is necessary. Our games are designed for both beginners and experienced players.
Are the escape rooms private?
Yes. Our escape rooms are private experiences reserved for your group when you book.
Can we leave the room whenever we want?
Yes. For safety and emergency purposes, participants may leave the room at any time.

Parties and Special Events

Can I host a birthday party at LockHouse?
Absolutely! Call (661) 505-3566 for information about birthday party packages, room rentals, and reservation options.
Do you offer corporate team-building events?
Yes! We offer options for special events, including team-building activities and party room rentals. Please call us to discuss your group's needs and available packages.
Can I bring food or drinks for a party?
Yes! Food and drinks are permitted in designated birthday party and event rental areas. However, food and drinks are not permitted inside the escape rooms.
Can I reserve the facility for a private event?
Yes, private event reservations may be available. Please contact us to discuss availability and rental options.

Rules and Policies

Can I take pictures or record videos inside the rooms?
No. Photography and video recording are not permitted inside our escape rooms to protect the puzzles and experience for future participants.
Are food and drinks allowed inside the escape rooms?
No. Food and drinks are not permitted inside the escape rooms. They may be allowed in designated party and event rental areas.
Are alcohol or recreational drugs allowed on the premises?
No. Alcohol and recreational drugs or substances, whether legal or illegal, are prohibited on the premises.
Are pets allowed?
Unfortunately, pets are not permitted inside LockHouse Escape Room. Please contact us regarding service animals and applicable accessibility requirements.
Are the rooms wheelchair accessible?
Accessibility varies by room, and some rooms may not meet ADA accessibility requirements. Please call (661) 505-3566 before booking so we can discuss your accessibility needs.
Do I need to sign a waiver before participating?
Yes. All required waivers must be completed before participating. You may be able to sign your waiver online during booking or complete it in person before your experience begins.
